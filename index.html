Before you leave the airport

  • Get the Property Irregularity Report (PIR) filled in at the baggage desk. Keep a copy and the reference number.
  • Photograph the PIR, your boarding pass and the bag tag stub.
  • Ask the airline in writing for hotel, meals and transport if you are stranded. Note the staff name and time.
  • Keep receipts for anything you have to buy.
